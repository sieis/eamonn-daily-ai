  - "AI shoppers: visits to retail sites from AI chat and search tools (shoppers clicking a link inside an AI answer) are up 127% so far this season. Salesforce separately measures AI-driven traffic to retailers up 104%"

  - "Salesforce's view: it forecasts $357 billion in global online sales for Cyber Week (November 24 to 30), with $75.8 billion influenced by AI tools, including AI agents that shop for people"
  - "Buy now, pay later: $6.1 billion so far, up 6.5%"
  - "Keep in mind: Adobe's percentages measure growth, not share. AI referrals are growing fast from a still-small base, and most purchases still happen the old-fashioned way"
whyItMatters: "More shoppers are starting their holiday search by asking an AI assistant what to buy instead of typing into Google or browsing Amazon. When the AI recommends a product, it links straight to a store. That means the stores AI tools know about, and can describe accurately, are picking up customers that used to come from search ads and social posts."
consultingAngle: "Spend 15 minutes this weekend asking ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude the questions your customers would ask, like 'best gift for a coffee lover under $50' or 'where can I buy handmade candles in [your town]'. Note whether your business appears and whether the details (prices, hours, shipping, return policy) are right. If you don't show up, make sure your website states plainly what you sell, your prices and your policies, keep your Google Business Profile and product listings current, and check that your online store isn't blocking AI shopping tools. Then tag AI traffic in your analytics (look for referrals from chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com and perplexity.ai) so you can see whether it's worth investing in after the holidays."
