keyDetails:
  - "The Enterprise AI Gateway is meant to handle sign-in, policy rules, routing, monitoring, usage tracking and audit trails for AI traffic. SAP calls it a premium product with no published price"
  - "Most coverage puts general availability in the first quarter of 2027, so it is a roadmap item, not something you can switch on today"
  - "Third-party agents will be able to call SAP's Joule agents through a controlled gateway that supports the A2A and MCP standards"
  - "Joule Studio, SAP's agent builder, offers 70+ AI models and new cost and behavior controls. Running custom agents on it is reported free for select customers through December 2026"
  - "An AI Governance Assistant is intended to help with EU AI Act requirements. Sources disagree on timing, so confirm with SAP"
whyItMatters: "As companies hand more work to AI agents, the worry shifts from 'can it do the task?' to 'who is watching what it does with our data?' SAP's announcement shows the largest business-software vendors treating that oversight as a product, not an afterthought."
consultingAngle: "You do not need SAP to take the lesson. Before any AI agent touches customer or financial data, decide who approves what it can access, how its actions are logged, and who can switch it off. If you run SAP, ask your rep which parts are live now versus 2027, and what the gateway will cost. If you do not, ask the same questions of whatever AI tools you already use."
