consultingAngle: "This is a good reality check to bring into any conversation about AI 'running' parts of your business: today's AI agents are genuinely useful as capable assistants handling defined tasks with a human checking the output, but handing over open-ended judgment calls — hiring, customer-facing honesty, pricing, and staff management — is still a live experiment, not a proven playbook. Use that distinction to set expectations with clients or your own team about where AI should assist versus where a person still needs to be in the loop."
