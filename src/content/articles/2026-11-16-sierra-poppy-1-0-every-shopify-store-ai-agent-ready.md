keyDetails:
  - "What it is: a shared set of rules for how a customer's own AI assistant identifies itself to a business and what it is allowed to do there, such as check an order, look up a fare or start a purchase"
  - "Released November 13 as version 1.0, about five weeks after Sierra and Meta first proposed it. The Next Web notes the specification is still labelled a draft, so details can change"
  - "Shopify has enabled it for every merchant. Each store now publishes a Poppy file automatically, and Sierra says merchants do not need to do anything"
  - "Meta's Muse assistant checks for a Poppy file first on every website it visits. The Next Web reports more than 10 million people have set up Muse"
  - "Customers sign in through a standard login and choose what their agent can access. For shopping, the agent can browse as a guest or sign in, and must ask the customer before certain actions"
  - "More than 100 companies are now design partners, 60 of them new, including Delta, Hyatt, Intuit, Salesforce, Wix, Wayfair, Twilio and Zoom. Mercury, Synchrony and Turkish Airlines are already live"
  - "Not on the list: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Amazon. Neither announcement mentions support from them, and Amazon has been steering shoppers toward its own assistant"
  - "Businesses not on Shopify can set it up themselves using a free kit and checker at personalagentprotocol.org"
whyItMatters: "More people are asking an AI assistant to do their shopping and customer service chores for them. Until now, each assistant had to guess its way around each website, and businesses had no clean way to tell a legitimate assistant from a bot. A shared standard is how that gets sorted out. This one moved from a proposal to being switched on across millions of online stores in about five weeks, which is fast."
consultingAngle: "If you run a Shopify store, you are already part of this whether you chose it or not. That is mostly good news, because AI assistants can now find your products and start a checkout without stumbling. It is worth ten minutes to look in your Shopify admin for the agent settings so you know what is switched on. Neither announcement explains how a merchant changes or turns off the defaults, so ask Shopify support if you cannot find it. Then check the basics an agent will read: accurate prices, stock levels, shipping costs and return policy. If you are not on Shopify, ask your website, booking or payments provider whether they plan to support Poppy before you spend money building anything. And keep in mind this covers Meta's assistant today. ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude have not signed on, so it is one door for AI customers and not the only one."
