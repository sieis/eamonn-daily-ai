consultingAngle: "If you're evaluating agent tools, ask vendors which AI model sits underneath and what it costs per task, because open models like this are putting downward pressure on prices. A practical first step is a small pilot on one repetitive process, such as reading scanned purchase orders, and compare accuracy and cost against what you use now."
