whyItMatters: "AI agents work by connecting to your accounts: email, messages, banking and more. That's what makes them useful, and it's also what makes a bug serious. When an agent gets confused about whose information it's looking at, private details can end up in front of the wrong person. Even if this was limited to test versions, it's a reminder that these products are still new."
consultingAngle: "Before anyone in your business connects an AI agent to work email, the company bank account or customer records, ask three questions: what exactly can it read, where is that data stored, and what happens if it mixes up accounts? Personal agent apps like Muse are built for consumers, not businesses, so keep them away from client and financial data. Prefer business plans that come with admin controls and a data-processing agreement, and give agents the narrowest access the task needs. Add a line to your AI policy saying staff shouldn't link personal AI agents to company accounts."
