keyDetails:
  - "Announced November 12 as a plugin for Claude Team and Enterprise plans. Setup starts by typing 'Set us up': Claude asks which systems you use, tests the connections and tells you which skills cannot fully run yet"
  - "17 skills across sales, quality, operations, supply chain and design. Examples include quoting a new job against past work, drafting a corrective action report after a failed inspection, diagnosing a breakdown from sensor data and repair notes, and preparing supplier requests then comparing the bids that come back"
  - "Connects to Oracle NetSuite (orders, inventory, job costs), Infor (purchase orders and stock), Autodesk Fusion (design models) and Microsoft 365 (files, email, reports)"
  - "No connector for your system? Anthropic says you can save regular exports to Microsoft 365 and Claude will work from those"
  - "Infor is releasing its own plugin that lets Claude take actions such as releasing purchase orders, but only after a user approves and only within that user's Infor permissions"
  - "Anthropic's example: when a steel shipment is delayed, a buyer asks which orders are affected and where else steel is available, then approves a purchase order with another supplier and a revised production schedule"
  - "Claude does not run machines or equipment. Every skill ends with a person reviewing the work before it reaches a customer or supplier, and figures can be traced back to the source data"
whyItMatters: "Most AI announcements are aimed at office work like email and slides. This one is built for the paperwork around the shop floor: the quotes, inspection reports and supplier back-and-forth that eat hours at small and mid-sized manufacturers. It is a sign that AI companies are now packaging their tools for specific industries, so owners do not have to work out from scratch how a general chatbot fits their business."
consultingAngle: "Quoting is the best place for most job shops to start. It is repetitive, it depends on knowing what similar jobs cost last time, and slow quotes lose work. Before switching anything on, check three things. First, are your past jobs and costs actually in NetSuite or another connected system, or scattered across spreadsheets and people's heads? The AI can only compare against data it can reach. Second, decide who signs off. Anthropic builds in a human review step, so name the estimator or manager who approves every quote and every purchase order. Third, run it side by side for a few weeks: have your estimator quote as normal, compare the AI's draft, and only lean on it once the numbers consistently match. The plugin requires a Team or Enterprise plan, so price the seats against the hours your estimators spend today."
