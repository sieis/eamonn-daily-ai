keyDetails:
  - "Generally available November 16 after a short preview that began November 11. Offered through Alibaba's QwenCloud service and its own apps"
  - "Price: $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, down from $2 and $6. A token is roughly three-quarters of a word. Artificial Analysis lists the typical price for comparable models at $2 and $10"
  - "Alibaba says the model matches or beats the previous version while using up to 50% fewer tokens, which it says cut the average cost of a work task in its own tests by about 62%, from $4.43 to $1.67"
  - "It can read text, images and video, search the web, and take in very long material in one go (a 1 million token window, roughly several long books)"
  - "It works with software written for OpenAI's or Anthropic's models, so developers can test it without rebuilding their tools"
  - "Not open: Qwen4-Max itself cannot be downloaded. Alibaba says smaller Qwen4 models will get open-weight releases, with no schedule. The existing open Qwen3.8 models remain available"
  - "Treat the performance claims with caution. The benchmarks are Alibaba's own, including a test it designed, and independent results are not in yet. Early testers reported it was stronger on new projects than on editing existing work"
whyItMatters: "You may never use Qwen directly, but releases like this are why AI keeps getting cheaper.
