ICSC found 65% of Thanksgiving-weekend shoppers plan to use AI tools, mostly to compare prices and research products, but only 9% plan to let AI complete a purchase. The National Retail Federation found 59% plan to use AI, mainly for product recommendations and gift ideas."

keyDetails:
  - "ICSC survey: 65% of U.S. adults shopping Thanksgiving Day through Cyber Monday plan to use AI tools or assistants, up from 63% last year. That rises to 77% of Millennials and 76% of Gen Z. Survey of 1,007 U.S. adults, November 9 to 11"
  - "How they'll use it (ICSC): comparing prices (31%), researching products and features (23%), checking whether something is in stock (19%) and finding deals (19%). Only 9% plan to have AI complete purchases for them"
  - "Stores still matter: 84% plan to shop in a physical store, the highest level since 2019, and 40% call themselves value-seekers who shop wherever prices are lowest, up from 34%"
  - "NRF survey: 59% of holiday shoppers plan to use AI over Thanksgiving weekend, most often for product recommendations (32%) and gift ideas (31%), followed by gift lists, budget tracking and scanning stores for the best price (25% each). Survey of 7,956 adults by Prosper Insights & Analytics, published November 19 (https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/183-million-consumers-expected-to-shop-during-thanksgiving-weekend)"
  - "Crowds: NRF expects 182.7 million people to shop over Thanksgiving weekend, down slightly from 186.9 million last year. ICSC puts planned spending at $124 billion"
  - "Context: Adobe has measured visits to retail sites from AI tools up 127% so far this season"
whyItMatters: "For most shoppers, AI has become a smarter search engine: a way to compare prices, find gift ideas and check what's in stock, not a robot that spends your money. People still want to make the final call themselves, and most are still heading to physical stores. So the AI's job this season is mostly to decide which stores and products people consider in the first place."
consultingAngle: "Your goal this weekend is to be the answer when someone asks an AI 'where can I get this nearby?' or 'what's a good gift under $50?' That means accurate prices and stock levels on your website and Google Business Profile, clear product descriptions, current holiday hours, and visible reviews, since AI tools pull from all of these. Because comparing prices is the top use, an AI will happily point out a cheaper competitor, so make your value obvious: bundles, free local pickup, gift wrapping or a price-match promise. Don't rush to build checkout for AI agents yet. Fewer than 1 in 10 shoppers want that today, while getting found is what pays now."
