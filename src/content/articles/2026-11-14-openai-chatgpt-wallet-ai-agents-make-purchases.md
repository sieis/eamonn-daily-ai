summary: "OpenAI's new ChatGPT Wallet lets its 'dots' AI agents complete purchases with your saved card. It is limited to U.S. Pro subscribers for now, every purchase needs your approval, and OpenAI has published guidance for merchants who want agents to be able to buy from them."

keyDetails:
  - "Announced November 13. Available first to U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 or older with a U.S.-issued Visa, Mastercard or American Express card"
  - "How it works: you add a card once, ask your dot (OpenAI's personal AI agent) to find something, review the items and total, then approve with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint"
  - "The agent never sees your card number. The card networks issue a one-time payment token that only works for that single transaction"
  - "OpenAI says card rewards and dispute protections stay in place, and it is not taking a cut of purchases at launch"
  - "Not available yet: letting the agent buy without asking. OpenAI says it plans to add pre-approval with spending limits, purchase categories and time windows"
  - "No list of participating stores was published. Merchants and card issuers can still decline a purchase, and some companies, including United Airlines and Amazon, restrict outside AI agents"
  - "For businesses: OpenAI released a Commerce Partner Playbook with guidance on making sure your website does not accidentally block its agents"
whyItMatters: "Until now, AI assistants could research and recommend, but you still had to type in your card and click buy. This closes that last step. It starts small and keeps a person approving every purchase, but it shows where shopping is heading: customers describing what they want and letting an assistant handle the checkout."
consultingAngle: "If you sell online, some of your future customers will be AI agents shopping on a person's behalf. Three things are worth checking now. First, ask whoever runs your website whether security settings or bot blockers turn away AI agents, and decide on purpose whether you want that. Second, make sure prices, stock levels, shipping costs and return policies are stated in plain text on the page, because an agent cannot buy what it cannot read. Third, if staff use ChatGPT with a company card, set a rule before this feature spreads: who may approve agent purchases and up to what amount. The wallet is narrow today, so there is no need to rebuild anything. It is a good moment to find out where your checkout stands."
