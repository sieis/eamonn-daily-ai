keyDetails:
  - "What it is: Mailchimp, owned by Intuit, is a widely used email and SMS marketing platform. Analytics AI is a conversational assistant built into it"
  - "Who it's for: small businesses and online stores that send email campaigns but do not have time to dig through reports or build dashboards"
  - "What it does: you ask questions like 'Why did my open rate drop last week?' and it points to factors such as send time, audience segment or seasonality, then recommends next steps. It can connect sales to specific emails and pulls store data from Shopify, WooCommerce and Wix"
  - "Also announced: integrations with Claude, Wix and WooCommerce, plus a beta AI Segment Builder that creates audience groups from a plain-language description"
  - "Direction, not product: Mailchimp says this is the foundation for AI that plans and runs campaigns on its own. That is a stated goal, not a shipped feature"
  - "Availability: pricing and plan eligibility for Analytics AI were not clearly published in what we found, and some Mailchimp AI tools are limited to higher plans. Check your account and Mailchimp's plans page"
whyItMatters: "Most small business owners already have the data they need to improve their marketing, but reading reports is a chore and nobody has time. Being able to just ask 'what worked and what should I do next?' lowers the barrier. The answers are only as good as the data behind them, so treat them as a starting point to check, not an instruction."
consultingAngle: "Example: a shop owner sends a weekly promotion and notices sales dipped. Instead of exporting spreadsheets, they ask the assistant what changed. If it points to a new send time or one weak audience segment, they can test a fix the same day, saving perhaps an hour of report-reading each week. Before relying on it, make sure your store is connected so revenue is attributed correctly, and verify any recommendation on a small send before rolling it out to your full list. If you use a different email platform, ask whether it offers something similar before switching."
