date: 2026-11-14
summary: "Hackers who broke into Fireworks AI, a company that runs AI models for other software makers, took chat transcripts from a Notion AI feature. Notion says fewer than 1% of workspaces are affected, but some transcripts contained API keys and other credentials users had typed into the chat."

keyDetails:
  - "Fireworks AI hosts AI models that other software companies build into their products. It says an attacker got in through its cloud provider and reached a development system holding some customer data"
  - "Notion was one of those customers. The stolen data was Notion AI chat transcripts that had been processed for a feature called Insights, including what users typed and what the AI answered"
  - "Notion says fewer than 1% of workspaces are affected and that most transcripts carry no label showing which user or company they came from. The text itself can still reveal who wrote it"
  - "Some messages contained API keys, which are the digital passwords that let software connect to other services. Notion revoked the exposed Notion keys on November 11 and reported other companies' keys to their issuers on November 12"
  - "Notion cut its connection to Fireworks on November 3, deleted its data there, switched off the Insights feature and contacted affected customers on November 9"
  - "Notion's advice: if you were not contacted, no action is needed. If you ever pasted passwords or keys into Notion AI, change them"
  - "TechCrunch reports this is Fireworks' second security incident in two months. Fireworks says its main services with no data retention were not affected and the investigation continues"
whyItMatters: "Most people think about whether they trust the app in front of them. This is a reminder that an AI feature often passes your words to other companies behind the scenes, and a break-in at any of them can expose what you typed. Notion itself was not hacked, yet its customers' conversations still leaked."
consultingAngle: "Two habits would have limited the damage here, and both cost nothing. First, never paste passwords, API keys, bank details or customer records into an AI chat box, in any tool. Make that a written rule for your team, because a chat can be stored and passed along in ways you cannot see. Second, for the software your business depends on, ask the vendor which outside companies process your data for AI features. Most publish this as a 'subprocessor' list. If you use Notion, check your admin email for a notice sent around November 9, and change any credentials that may have been typed into Notion AI. If you were not contacted, Notion says you do not need to do anything."
