consultingAngle: "If you're in the 'doesn't apply to us' camp, test that claim against one boring task: answering the same customer question, writing invoices or summarizing meeting notes. If one of those saves an hour a week, it applies. If you're already using AI but not seeing results, measure one outcome, such as hours saved or response time, rather than expecting sales to jump. Adoption without a target is how a business ends up paying for tools nobody uses."
