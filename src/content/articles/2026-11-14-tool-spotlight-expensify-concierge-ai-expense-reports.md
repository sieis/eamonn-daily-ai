whyItMatters: "Expense paperwork is the kind of chore that gets put off until tax season, when receipts are faded or lost. Letting software capture each one in the moment keeps the books accurate and removes a job nobody wants."
consultingAngle: "Example: a five-person contracting company has each crew lead buying materials most days. Instead of a shoebox of receipts at month end, each lead snaps a photo at the counter. The app reads it, tags it to the right job category and sends it to the owner for a one-tap approval, then on to QuickBooks. A task that took the owner two or three hours a month becomes a few minutes of review. To keep it reliable, set clear categories up front, spot-check a handful of scanned receipts each month against the originals, and have a person approve anything over an amount you choose. AI reading a crumpled receipt can still get a number wrong."
