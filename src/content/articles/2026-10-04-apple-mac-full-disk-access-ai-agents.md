keyDetails:
  - "Full Disk Access is the Mac setting that lets an app see nearly everything: email, Messages, Safari history, Home data and Time Machine backups"
  - "Apple says that as AI agents become more capable and autonomous, the risk of granting that access 'will grow substantially'"
  - "Future macOS updates will require 'very explicit user action' so people understand the risk before approving"
  - "Apple hasn't yet said exactly how the new controls will work or when they will arrive"
  - "The announcement follows a reported incident in which a journalist said Meta's Muse agent appeared to know the contents of their private messages without permission being granted"
whyItMatters: "AI assistants that 'just handle things' for you need access to your files, email and messages to do it. That convenience is also the risk: if you click 'allow' without thinking, you may be handing a program your whole digital life. Apple stepping in shows even the platform makers think the permission prompts people click through today aren't enough."
consultingAngle: "Before the new controls arrive, do a quick audit. If you manage company Macs with a device-management tool, ask your IT provider how it will handle the new prompts so agents your team relies on don't break unexpectedly."
