  - "McKinsey frames the finding as companies being 'on the road to ROI' rather than having arrived — the gap is attributed to AI still not being embedded deeply enough into actual workflows to move the bottom line"

whyItMatters: "If you've felt like everyone else is already cashing in on AI while you're still figuring it out, this is the reality check: even among large companies pouring money into AI, most still can't point to it actually improving their bottom line. Buying the tools is the easy part — getting real value out of them is where almost everyone is stuck."
consultingAngle: "This is exactly the data point to bring up when a vendor or consultant promises fast, dramatic ROI from an AI rollout — even McKinsey's own survey base of major companies isn't seeing it consistently yet.
