whyItMatters: "If you publish anything online, such as a blog, product pages or a price list, AI companies may use it to build their products. Until now, saying no meant learning a different rule for each company. A single shared setting would make that choice simple, though it only works if the companies follow it."
consultingAngle: "Ask whoever runs your website to review your robots.txt file this quarter. Decide first whether you want AI tools to learn from your content or to quote it in answers, because blocking can reduce how often you show up in AI search results. Many businesses will want to allow some pages, like product info, and block others, like gated guides or customer resources. Write that decision down as a simple content policy so it does not get settled by accident."
