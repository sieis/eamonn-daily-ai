consultingAngle: "You can copy the approach at any size. Start everyone on a basic assistant, watch who actually uses it, then build deeper workflows for the teams that do, such as purchasing or sales follow-up. Track something concrete before you start, like hours spent on weekly reports or time to answer a customer quote, so you can show a result later. Ask any vendor pitching you a big rollout number what has actually changed for the people using it."
