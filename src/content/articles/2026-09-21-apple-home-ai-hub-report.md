keyDetails:
  - "According to Bloomberg's reporting, the device, internally code-named J490, is a square touchscreen display designed to sit on a speaker base or mount to a wall, centralizing smart home controls, video calls, media, and a far more capable Siri in one shared household device"
  - "It is expected to use facial recognition to identify who is standing in front of it and tailor what it shows to that specific person, separate profiles for separate family members or, potentially, employees"
  - "The device would run on the same underlying Gemini-powered technology behind the new Siri, processed through Apple's own Private Cloud Compute rather than sending data to Google directly"
  - "The launch has reportedly slipped multiple times, originally planned for 2025, it is now expected sometime between October 2026 and early 2027, largely because the new Siri AI was not ready sooner, with a rumored price around $350"
  - "This follows Apple executives framing the iPhone as the company's current intelligent personal hub at its September event; this device would be Apple's first attempt to extend that same idea into a dedicated home device"
whyItMatters: "Amazon and Google have sold AI-powered smart displays for years, but Apple sitting out of that category has been notable given how central AI has become to its pitch this year. If this device ships, it signals Apple sees AI moving from something you talk to on your phone to something built into a shared screen in your kitchen, waiting room, or storefront, closer to how Amazon and Google already treat their own hubs."
consultingAngle: "This is not available yet, so there is nothing to buy today, but if you run any kind of business with a physical location, a waiting room, retail counter, salon, or office lobby, it is worth keeping on your radar as a potential low-cost AI reception or information display once it ships, the same way some businesses already use an Echo Show or Nest Hub for scheduling, FAQs, or check-in today. Worth a bookmark, not a budget line, for now."
