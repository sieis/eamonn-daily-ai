keyDetails:
  - "What changes: temporary Gemini chats, and chats made with history turned off, will be kept for about two months instead of 72 hours. Google has not given an exact number of days"
  - "Announced in a Google blog post on November 16. It rolls out globally in the Gemini app over the coming weeks"
  - "Google's reason: to 'improve safety and security as AI systems become more capable,' plus responding to users, handling feedback and protecting its services. Google did not say what prompted the change"
  - "Google says temporary chats are not used to train its AI models"
  - "The single 'Keep Activity' setting is being split in two: one switch for chat history and one for AI training. You can now keep your history without letting Google train on it"
  - "If you had Keep Activity off, both new switches start off. If you had it on, history stays on and you choose separately about training"
  - "For comparison: OpenAI says it may keep temporary ChatGPT chats for up to 30 days, and Anthropic keeps incognito Claude chats for 30 days"
  - "Not clear yet: whether this applies to Google Workspace business accounts. One privacy site says Workspace history-off chats stay at 72 hours under admin control, but Google's coverage does not address it"

Google says it won't be used to train AI, but it still exists and can be kept longer for legal or safety reasons."
