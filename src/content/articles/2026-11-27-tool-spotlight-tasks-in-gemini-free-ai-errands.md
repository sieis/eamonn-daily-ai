date: 2026-11-27
summary: "Google has made Tasks in Gemini, its AI agent formerly known as Gemini Spark, free in the U.S., Canada, Japan, Korea, Australia and India. Flip the Gemini app from Chat to Task and it can call businesses to book appointments, dig through bills, shop within a spending cap you set and work through multi-step errands. In the U.S. you can also text it or forward it emails."
source: 'https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/'

keyDetails:
  - "What it is: an AI agent inside the free Gemini app that carries out multi-step errands using your connected email, calendar and payment methods. Each task runs in its own isolated virtual computer tied to your account. It used to require a paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plan"
  - "Who it's for: busy solo owners, freelancers and small teams who lose hours to phone calls, scheduling and small purchases. It's a personal assistant, so it works best on your own errands rather than as a team system"
  - "What it does: calls businesses to book appointments that fit your calendar, reviews bills to find out why a charge was high, shops and checks out (including Instacart grocery orders), and adds dates from emails to your calendar"
  - "How to use it: in the Gemini app, switch the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task. In the U.S. you can also forward emails to gemini@google.com or set up texting with Gemini under Settings, then Channels"
  - "Safety controls: Gemini tells businesses it's an AI when it calls, and businesses can opt out. It asks before it hits 'Buy', stays under a hard spending cap you set, and won't share payment or account details unless you approve. Purchases use Google Pay with Visa and Mastercard, and receipts land in Google Wallet"
  - "Limits: free in six countries (not the UK or EU). Calling isn't available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana or Nebraska. Free use has unpublished usage limits, Google warns tasks can fail, and on some sites it hands the final click back to you"
whyItMatters: "Most AI chatbots only tell you how to do something. This one actually does it: it makes the phone call, compares prices and fills out the checkout, while you approve the important steps. Making it free puts a real AI errand-runner in the pocket of anyone with a phone in these countries."
consultingAngle: "Example: a solo landscaper needs to book a truck service, reorder trimmer line at the best price and confirm three supplier pickups for next week. That's usually 45 minutes of hold music and browser tabs. With Tasks, they type 'Call the two closest dealers and book an oil change for my truck on a morning I'm free next week, and find 0.095 trimmer line under $40 for delivery by Friday.' Gemini makes the calls, checks the calendar, and comes back with a booking and a cart to approve. Start with low-risk jobs like appointments and price checks, set a small spending cap, and keep business purchases on a dedicated card. Also note the flip side: AI assistants like this will increasingly call your business, so make sure whoever answers the phone knows how to handle an AI caller booking on a customer's behalf."
