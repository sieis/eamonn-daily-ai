keyDetails:
  - "At Apple's September 9 event, John Ternus delivered his first keynote as CEO since succeeding Tim Cook, opening with the iPhone 18 Pro and a new in-house C2 modem before the closing 'one more thing': the iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable phone, unfolding to a single continuous 7.6-inch screen and priced from $1,999 up to $3,199 for the top configuration"
  - "The Duo supports Apple Pencil input for the first time on an iPhone, ships with a 48MP camera system and 4K/120fps video, and is described as the thinnest iPhone ever made; preorders open October 16 with availability October 23"
  - "Apple also introduced the Watch Series 12 and Watch Ultra 4 with a new S11 chip powering 'Audio Intelligence' — on-device sound recognition for things like alarms, doorbells, or a crying baby, plus a 'Live Rewind' feature that can rewind and transcribe the last 15 seconds of audio — and a new 'Health Age' and 0-10 daily 'Readiness' score"
  - "Apple emphasized that these new AI features process on-device inside a secure hardware enclave by default, only reaching out to the cloud when necessary — a deliberate contrast to competitors whose AI features route most requests to a data center"
  - "This is the first major hardware cycle under Ternus, and early coverage is reading it as a signal that Apple intends to compete on new form factors and privacy-first AI processing rather than chasing a general-purpose chatbot feature race"

It also marks a real leadership transition at one of the most influential companies in tech, worth knowing about regardless of your industry."
