consultingAngle: "The gap between using AI and depending on it is where the money is. Pick one repeatable process, such as quoting, scheduling, invoicing follow-up or customer replies, and write down the steps, who does them and how long they take. Keep in mind this is a survey of program alumni, who tend to be more engaged than the typical owner."
