  - "Just 27% have rules on what company or customer data can be entered into AI tools, and only 25% have any procedure for checking AI-generated output before it's actually used in a business decision"

  - "82% of business owners say they personally need more information or resources to protect their business against AI-enabled cyberattacks — the gap isn't reluctance to learn, it's a lack of a starting point"

The risk isn't hypothetical or future — it's already showing up in the numbers for businesses very similar to yours."

It costs almost nothing to put in place and directly targets the exact failure points — no policy, no training, no data rules — that 31% of businesses already got burned by."
