They answer staff questions about IT, HR, finance and legal from your existing documents, inside Slack, Microsoft Teams or an internal help center. Best suited to teams of roughly 50 and up."

keyDetails:
  - "What it is: Zendesk is a well-known customer-support platform. Its employee service AI agents point the same technology inward, answering questions from your own staff rather than your customers"
  - "Who it's for: growing and mid-sized companies where a small IT, HR or finance team is buried in repeat questions. Very small teams may find it more than they need"
  - "What it does: agents connect to knowledge sources such as Google Drive, SharePoint and Confluence, respect existing permissions from identity tools like Okta or Active Directory, and can take actions, not only answer. They run in Slack, Microsoft Teams or an internal help center"
  - "Pricing and availability: Zendesk says AI agents are included in Employee Service Suite plans, with a set number of resolution credits, and that after general availability fees apply to resolutions drawn from connected external knowledge, while answers from the Zendesk Help Center stay free. Rollout runs November 3 to December 1. Confirm current terms with Zendesk"
  - "Claim to treat with care: Zendesk marketing cites a 30% automated resolution rate for employee requests. That is vendor-reported and not independently verified"
whyItMatters: "Every company has the same quiet time drain: employees asking 'how do I reset this?', 'where is the PTO policy?' or 'who approves this expense?' over and over. An AI agent that answers from your own documents, in the chat app people already use, removes those interruptions and lets your most knowledgeable people focus on harder work."
consultingAngle: "Example: a 60-person company where the office manager and one IT person field about 25 repeat questions a week at 10 minutes each. If an AI agent handled even 30% of them, that is roughly 75 minutes back every week, plus faster answers for staff at any hour. Before buying anything, write down your 20 most common internal questions and check whether the answers exist in writing, because the agent is only as good as your documents. Start with one department and review the agent's answers weekly. If you are under about 25 people, a shared FAQ document plus a general AI assistant may do the job at lower cost."
