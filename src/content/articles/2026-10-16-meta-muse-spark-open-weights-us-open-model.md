keyDetails:
  - "Meta says Muse Spark Open is built for coding and multi-step 'agent' work, with 816 billion total parameters (about 42 billion active at a time) and a context window of up to 1 million tokens, roughly several long books at once"
  - "The model files are on Hugging Face, and you can also use it through Amazon SageMaker, Google Cloud Model Garden and Microsoft Foundry, plus hosts such as Together AI, Fireworks, Groq and OpenRouter"
  - "Per press coverage, the license has 'limited commercial terms': building it into your own product is free, but a company that sells it as a hosted service and has passed $20 million in revenue over 12 months needs a separate agreement. Military and critical-infrastructure uses are banned, and products must carry a 'Built with Muse Spark Open' label"
  - "This is a step down from Meta's best models: Muse Wonder stays closed and is sold only through Meta's own API. Meta's Alexandr Wang said the company will generally not open-source its most powerful systems"
  - "Benchmark claims come from Meta. Independent testing will take a few weeks, so treat the 'most capable US open model' label as a claim until others confirm it"
whyItMatters: "Most AI tools you use are rented: you pay a company, and your data goes through its servers. 'Open-weight' models can be downloaded and run by anyone, which can mean lower costs, more privacy and no risk of a vendor changing the rules on you. This release means that option is getting much closer to the quality of the paid tools, and a big U.S. company is behind it."
consultingAngle: "You probably won't run an 816-billion-parameter model yourself, but your software vendors might, and it can lower prices for you. If you handle sensitive customer, patient or financial data, ask your IT provider whether an open model hosted in your own cloud account could keep that data in-house. Before anything is built on it, have someone read the license, especially the revenue threshold and the labeling rule, and compare real results on your own tasks against the paid tools you use today."
