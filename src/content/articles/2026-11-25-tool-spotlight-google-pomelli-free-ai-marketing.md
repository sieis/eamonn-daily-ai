summary: "Pomelli is a free Google Labs tool built with Google DeepMind that scans your business website to learn your brand's colors, fonts and tone, then drafts on-brand social posts, ads and web graphics. Its Photoshoot feature turns a plain phone photo of a product into studio-style or lifestyle images, and Animate turns images into short videos. It's a handy way to fill a holiday content calendar without a designer."

keyDetails:
  - "What it is: a free AI marketing experiment from Google Labs, built with Google DeepMind, designed for small and medium businesses"
  - "Who it's for: owners and small teams who need a steady stream of social posts and ads but don't have a designer or marketing agency"
  - "How it works: paste in your website address and Pomelli builds a 'Business DNA' profile of your brand's voice, fonts, colors and images. It then suggests campaign ideas, or works from your own prompt, and produces editable, downloadable assets for social media, websites and ads"
  - "Photoshoot: upload an unpolished product photo, pick a studio or lifestyle template, and it generates professional-looking images in your brand style, which you can refine with text edits and style reference images"
  - "Animate: turns campaign images into short videos for stories and reels. Reviewers note text inside animated clips can come out distorted, so check before posting"
  - "Availability: English only, in the U.S., Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the U.K., Switzerland and much of Europe. You sign in with a Google account through Google Labs"
  - "Keep in mind: it's a Labs experiment, so features can change, and AI product images should still show your real product accurately. Don't let it invent details customers will expect when the box arrives"
whyItMatters: "Good-looking marketing used to mean paying a photographer and a designer, or settling for blurry phone pictures. Pomelli gives a small business a free way to produce consistent, professional-looking posts that actually match its own brand, in minutes instead of days."
consultingAngle: "Example: a bakery wants a week of holiday posts for Instagram and Facebook plus a Small Business Saturday ad. The owner pastes in the bakery's website, takes a quick phone photo of the gingerbread boxes on the counter, and asks Pomelli for a holiday pre-order campaign. It returns on-brand images, a lifestyle shot of the boxes on a festive table and caption ideas in the bakery's voice. Instead of three or four hours with a design app, or a few hundred dollars for a freelancer, the owner spends about 30 minutes picking favorites, fixing the wording and scheduling them."
