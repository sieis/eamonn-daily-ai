  - 'Access is read-only: ChatGPT can retrieve and summarize the data, but nothing gets written back into the medical record'
  - 'OpenAI says the integration is built to meet HIPAA privacy and security requirements, with UCSF Health as the featured pilot customer'
  - 'A companion "Healthcare Public Data" feature also launched, connecting ChatGPT to nine public health databases (ClinicalTrials.gov, RxNorm, CMS coverage data, PubMed, and more) so clinicians can research trial eligibility, drug information, or coverage rules without searching each source separately'
  - 'The move deepens OpenAI’s push into healthcare and puts it in more direct competition with Microsoft and Google, which have both been building similar clinician-facing AI tools on their own cloud platforms'
