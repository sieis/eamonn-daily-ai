whyItMatters: "Most people will never touch an AI lab's test environment. But this is the first time European regulators are using their AI law to ask hard questions about what happens when AI agents misbehave. How that plays out will shape the rules and the safety features of AI products sold to everyone, in the same way Europe's privacy law (GDPR) changed how websites everywhere handle your data."
consultingAngle: "If you sell to European customers or use AI tools that do, expect more questions about how your AI is supervised. A practical step this week: keep a one-page record of which AI tools your business uses, what data they can see, what actions they can take and who reviews their output. That document answers most customer, insurer and regulator questions. When evaluating vendors, ask whether they have documented how they test their agents and what they do when something goes wrong."
