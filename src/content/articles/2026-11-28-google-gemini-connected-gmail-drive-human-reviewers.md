date: 2026-11-28
summary: "After a researcher found the consumer Gemini app pulling email and file contents into answers without being asked, Google confirmed that a small share of conversations using connected Gmail and Drive can be read by trained human reviewers, sometimes including full emails. Google says this doesn't apply to business Workspace accounts, and you can disconnect your apps in a few clicks."

keyDetails:
  - "What the researcher found: testing a personal Google account with connected apps turned on, the consumer Gemini app sometimes searched Gmail or Drive without being asked. In one test, a question about whether an internet bill seemed high brought back payment amounts and confirmation numbers. Some answers quoted whole emails, headers included"
  - "What Google says: this is 'standard behavior for someone who had voluntarily connected their apps.' Connecting apps is off by default, and you can disconnect at any time without losing Gemini's core features"
  - "Human review: if you connect apps while activity saving is on, Google says a small subset of conversations may be reviewed by trained people, including outside service providers. Reviewers may see summaries or excerpts, or a complete email or file if it's short or especially relevant. Google says it separates these conversations from your account first"
  - "How long it's kept: reviewed conversations can be retained for up to three years, even after you delete your activity, according to Google's Gemini privacy hub"
  - "What changed: in 2023 Google said Gmail, Docs and Drive content used by Bard would not be seen by human reviewers. On October 16, 2026, it updated that post to say newer connected features may involve limited human review and training"
  - "Business accounts are different: Google says customer data on Workspace Business, Enterprise and Education plans is not reviewed by humans or used to train its main AI models. Your organization's settings and agreements decide how data is handled"
  - "To disconnect: at gemini.google.com go to Settings, then Personal Intelligence, then Connected Apps, then Google Workspace, choose Disconnect, and delete past conversations separately"
whyItMatters: "Connecting your inbox to an AI assistant feels like giving it a filing cabinet only it can open. Google's own fine print says that's not quite true on a personal account: a person may occasionally read what Gemini pulled out of your email, and that copy can stick around for years. This doesn't mean anyone is reading your email right now. It means you should decide on purpose what you connect."
consultingAngle: "This is a free-versus-paid line every business should understand. Many small business owners run their company out of a personal Gmail account, and on that account connected-app data can go to human review. On a paid Google Workspace business plan, Google says it doesn't. If client contracts, invoices or employee details live in a personal Gmail, either move business email to Workspace or keep Gmail and Drive disconnected from consumer Gemini. Write a one-paragraph AI policy for your staff: personal AI accounts are fine for public information, but anything about customers, money or people goes only into company-managed tools."
