  - "Z.ai (formerly Zhipu AI) confirmed on September 17 that 'Ox Alpha' — an anonymous model that had quietly topped usage charts on the OpenRouter marketplace — was actually GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320-billion-parameter model (18 billion active) it had been running in stealth to gauge real-world reception before claiming it publicly"
  - "Every request served during that stealth trial ran on a cluster of more than 100,000 domestically produced Chinese AI accelerators rather than Nvidia chips — something Z.ai says no company had previously operated at this scale, at a per-token cost it claims is comparable to mainstream Nvidia hardware"
  - "GLM-5.3-Flash is priced at $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens — in the same ultra-low-cost bracket as DeepSeek — while approaching Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks, according to the company's own testing"
  - "Z.ai says much of the engineering work to stand up that 100,000-chip cluster was carried out by its own 'Infra Agent,' built on GLM-5.3, rather than human engineers alone — an early public example of an AI system helping build the very hardware pipeline it then runs on"
  - "The full GLM-5.3 model is open-weight under a custom license and the Flash variant under the permissive MIT license, both downloadable now on Hugging Face; Zhipu AI's Hong Kong-listed shares rose on the news"
