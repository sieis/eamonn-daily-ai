  - "Who it's for: small teams that already juggle requests, deadlines and handoffs across email and chat and want one place to track them"
  - "What it does: you describe a process, for example 'when a client request comes in, summarize it, assign it and set a due date', and AI Studio runs it for you"

consultingAngle: "Example: a design or consulting firm receives client requests through a form. An AI Studio workflow reads each request, tags it by type, assigns it to the right person and sets a due date, replacing 5 to 10 minutes of sorting per request. Start with one repetitive process, review the results for a couple of weeks, then expand. Watch the credit usage so costs stay predictable."
