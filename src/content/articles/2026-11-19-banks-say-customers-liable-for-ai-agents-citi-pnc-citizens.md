keyDetails:
  - "Citi, the third-largest U.S. bank, updated its online user agreement this week: customers are 'responsible for activities conducted through' third-party AI agents they connect to their accounts"
  - "That includes the worst case. If an AI tool leaks your login or is hacked, Citi says you are responsible for what the attackers do with your account"
  - "PNC changed its terms on November 18, and Citizens emailed customers this week saying they are on the hook for anything their AI assistant does"
  - "JPMorgan and Bank of America, the two largest U.S. banks, announced similar policies in October. Bank of America has asked customers not to share banking passwords with AI assistants"
  - "The rules cover outside tools: chatbots from the big AI labs and personal-finance apps that log in to your accounts to analyze spending, pay bills or move money"
  - "Citi's own built-in AI features are exempt. Using the bank's own tools does not add liability"
  - "Read-only tools are lower risk. Anthropic's personal-finance feature for Claude, launched earlier this month, is described as read-only and cannot move, manage or invest money"
whyItMatters: "AI assistants are starting to do real things with money: paying bills, buying things, moving funds. The big banks have now drawn a line. If you hand an outside AI tool the keys to your account and something goes wrong, the bank is telling you up front it may not cover the loss. Fraud protections that people take for granted may not apply when an AI agent you authorized was the one acting."
consultingAngle: "Treat bank access as the one place not to experiment. Don't paste business banking passwords into ChatGPT, Claude or any agent, and don't let a third-party tool log in as you. If you want AI help with cash flow, use read-only connections, export statements and upload them, or use the AI built into your bank or accounting software, which banks treat differently. Check which apps currently have access to your business accounts (most banks list them under security or connected apps) and remove anything you don't recognize or no longer use. If you have staff, put it in writing: no AI tool gets payment or transfer permissions without the owner's sign-off. And if you bank with Citi, PNC, Citizens, Chase or Bank of America, read the updated agreement before connecting any new tool."
