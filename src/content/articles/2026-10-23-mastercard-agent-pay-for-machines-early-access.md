keyDetails:
  - "Early access includes buyer-side and seller-side APIs, a developer toolkit and a testing sandbox. It is not commercially available yet"
  - "In Mastercard's demo, a marketing agency's AI agent running a campaign made nine separate payments to two merchants, AIsa and Cloudflare AI Gateway, all from one budget approved up front"
  - "It builds on x402, an open protocol that lets online services ask for payment, and can use cards, bank accounts and stablecoins"
  - "Pilot partners named include Cloudflare, Coinbase, Base, BVNK, Ant International, AIsa and Hype"

whyItMatters: "Today, AI assistants can research and recommend, but a human usually has to hand over a credit card. This is the plumbing that would let an assistant buy things for you, such as ad space or software tools, without you approving each purchase. The safety idea is a spending cap you set before the agent starts."
consultingAngle: "You do not need to do anything now, but it is worth deciding how you would want this to work. In the meantime, keep company cards away from experimental agents, and prefer tools that let you set hard spending limits."
