consultingAngle: "If anyone on your team already pays for Grok, Cursor or X Premium separately, check whether the bundle costs less. Before you move a whole team over, ask what 'usage' means in practice: how many requests per month, and what happens when you hit the cap. Spending caps are a useful safety feature for a business, because they stop a surprise bill, but a hard cap can also stall a project midweek. Pick the cheapest tier for a two-week trial and track how often people hit the limit."
