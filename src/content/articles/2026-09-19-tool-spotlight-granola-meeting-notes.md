keyDetails:
  - "Granola is a Mac and Windows desktop app that runs quietly during video calls and captures your computer's own system audio directly — no visible bot joins the meeting, so it works even on calls where outside apps aren't allowed or would look unprofessional to a client"
  - "Unlike notetakers that write a summary from scratch, Granola is built around your own shorthand: you type quick fragments during the call the way you always have, and it uses the transcript to expand those into full, organized notes the second you hang up"
  - "Before any meeting with an outside contact, it preps a 'Brief' pulling who's attending and what you discussed last time from your calendar and past notes, and can push finished summaries straight into Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Attio, or 8,000+ other apps via Zapier"
  - "Pricing: a free Basic plan capped at 25 notes total — not per month, ever — a Business plan at $14/user/month with unlimited history and integrations, and an Enterprise tier at $35/user/month with SSO and org-wide retention"
  - "Granola raised a $125 million Series C in March 2026 at a $1.5 billion valuation and has been repositioning from 'meeting notetaker' toward what it calls an 'AI context layer' — an MCP integration now lets Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI tools pull directly from your meeting history when you ask them a question"
whyItMatters: "A huge amount of small-business time disappears into meetings and the scramble to remember what was actually said or promised afterward. Tools like this don't replace judgment — they just make sure the record of a conversation survives past the conversation itself, which is exactly the kind of unglamorous task AI is genuinely good at handling well right now."
consultingAngle: "Before your next recurring client call, open Granola's Brief instead of digging through old emails to remember what you promised last time — it takes seconds. And if you already use Claude or ChatGPT for business tasks, connecting it to your Granola meeting history means you can ask 'what did we agree to do for this client last month' and get a real, sourced answer instead of scrolling through old threads. Worth setting up once and reusing on every repeat client relationship."
