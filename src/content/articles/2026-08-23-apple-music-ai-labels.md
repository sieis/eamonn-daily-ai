keyDetails:
  - "Record labels and distributors will be required to tag any track, artwork, composition, or music video that was 'materially generated using AI' before it goes up on Apple Music"
  - "Those tags will become visible to listeners as a 'Made With AI' label later in 2026 — Apple hasn't given an exact launch date"
  - 'Apple introduced the underlying AI Transparency Tag requirement earlier this year; this move makes those tags visible to the public for the first time'
  - 'Apple joins other platforms, including Spotify and YouTube, that are rolling out their own AI-disclosure rules as AI-generated music becomes more common'
whyItMatters: "AI-made music has gotten good enough that most listeners can't reliably tell the difference anymore, which has made artists and labels nervous about transparency. A visible label from a platform as big as Apple Music is a real step toward giving listeners — and the artists competing for their attention — a clear, simple answer to 'was this made by a person?'"
consultingAngle: "If your business uses AI-generated music or audio anywhere in your marketing — ads, social videos, in-store playlists, podcast intros — this is an early look at where disclosure requirements are headed across every platform, not just Apple Music. Getting comfortable labeling AI-generated content now, even where it isn't required yet, is cheap insurance against a rule change later."
