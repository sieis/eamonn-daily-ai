keyDetails:
  - "Claude Sheets (beta) builds and edits spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs, often from one prompt. Each edit is outlined so you can see what changed. Anthropic says features are basic for now and you can export to .xlsx or CSV"
  - "Claude Whiteboards (beta) is a shared board where a team brainstorms with Claude. It can turn a messy plan into a timeline with owners and dates, group ideas by theme and draft action items"
  - "Claude Data (generally available) lets teams ask questions of company data in tools such as BigQuery, Redshift and ClickHouse. It needs a data connector, and certified queries and live sharing are still in beta"
  - "Claude Meetings (beta, Team and Enterprise plans) joins a Google Meet call as a visible participant, announces itself in chat, and will not join calls with external guests unless an admin allows it, according to TechCrunch"
  - "All three launch on paid plans. They are on by default for Team plans. For Enterprise, Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default and Claude Data turns on by default on November 19, 2026"
whyItMatters: "Most people use AI by typing into a chat window and copying the answer somewhere else. These features put the AI inside the spreadsheet, the planning board and the meeting itself, which is where much of the work actually happens. Think of it as a helper that sits at the table instead of one you have to brief afterwards."
consultingAngle: "Example: a five-person marketing agency holds a Monday planning call. With Claude Meetings on, the plan, owners and deadlines are drafted during the conversation, and a Sheets tab with the campaign budget is ready when the call ends. That is a task someone usually spends 30 minutes on afterwards. Before turning it on, decide who is allowed to record or summarize calls, tell clients when an assistant is present, and have a person check any numbers in a generated spreadsheet before they go into a quote or report. Admins on Enterprise plans should review the new settings under Organization settings, since two of the three are off by default."
