  - 'Daily AI users are the only group who feel net-positive about job security from AI: 27% feel more secure versus 22% who feel less secure'
  - 'That flips for weekly users — just 10% feel more secure, while 25% feel less secure — and 37% of all workers say they never use AI at work'

whyItMatters: "A lot of headlines treat 'AI anxiety' as one big feeling everyone shares. This survey shows it isn't — the people using AI daily are actually the most confident about their own job security, while people using it occasionally, or not at all, feel the most uneasy. Familiarity seems to be the thing that calms the fear down, not avoidance."
consultingAngle: "If a business hasn't put anything in writing about whether junior employees can use AI tools — and how — that gap is its own source of confusion and inconsistency on a team, separate from any technical risk.
