whyItMatters: "Many businesses feel AI is saving time, but feeling is not the same as knowing. This survey suggests most leaders cannot yet show what they spend on AI or what they get back. For anyone paying for AI subscriptions, that is a good prompt to check whether the tools are earning their keep."
consultingAngle: "You do not need an enterprise dashboard to fix this. Pick one task AI now handles, such as drafting quotes or answering routine emails. Write down how long it took before and how long it takes now, then compare that saved time to what you pay for the tool. Review once a quarter and cancel what nobody uses. The survey also points to old systems as the main brake, so if your tools do not talk to each other, fixing that may pay off more than adding another AI subscription."
