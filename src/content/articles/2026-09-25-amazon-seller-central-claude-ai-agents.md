keyDetails:
  - "Amazon's new 'Selling Partner' plugin, in US beta, connects Claude (or Amazon's own 'Quick' assistant) directly to a seller's account, pulling in listings, inventory, sales analytics, and performance data so the AI can act on them"
  - "Setup takes about 60 seconds and requires no coding — a seller links their account once and can then ask Claude to reprice a listing, restock inventory, answer a customer message, or pull a sales report in plain English"
  - "Every action needs the seller's approval before it executes, and Amazon logs an audit trail of everything the AI does on the account"
  - "Amazon says seller data stays inside its own infrastructure rather than being shipped off to a third party for processing"
  - "To drive adoption, Amazon is bundling a free 12-month 'Quick Plus' subscription (through the end of 2026) for primary account holders who try it"
whyItMatters: "Millions of small businesses sell on Amazon, and most of them lose real hours every week to Seller Central's dashboards, spreadsheets exports, and back-and-forth customer messages. This is one of the first times a major platform has let an outside AI assistant — not just its own built-in tool — actually run day-to-day operations on someone's behalf, with a receipt for every action it takes."
consultingAngle: "For a client already selling on Amazon, this is worth testing this week rather than filing away: the free year of Quick Plus removes the cost objection, and the approval-required workflow means there's no real downside to trying it on a few routine tasks (restocking, price checks, answering FAQ-style messages) before trusting it with anything bigger. It's also a useful case study for the broader pitch that 'AI agent with an audit trail' is quickly becoming the standard business owners should expect from any tool asking for access to their store, their books, or their calendar."
