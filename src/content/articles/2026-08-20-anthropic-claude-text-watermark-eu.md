keyDetails:
  - "The watermark is built into which words Claude picks when more than one option would work equally well — it changes no formatting, adds no extra characters, and is invisible to a normal reader"
  - "Anthropic is using SynthID-Text, a technique Google DeepMind published in the journal Nature back in 2024, rather than building its own from scratch"
  - "Every Claude model released after August 2, 2026 carries the watermark, and it applies globally by default, whether or not the person using Claude is anywhere near the EU"
  - "The move follows Anthropic signing the EU's Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content in July 2026, alongside roughly 190 other signatories; the EU AI Act's own transparency rules took effect August 2 and carry fines up to €15 million or 3% of global revenue for non-compliance"
  - "Anthropic plans to release a detector so anyone can check whether a piece of text passed through Claude, but the company is upfront that a match confirms only that Claude touched the text at some point — not the full editing history after that"

Anthropic's response — a hidden mark baked into the writing itself rather than a visible tag you could delete — is the shape this is likely to take everywhere else too, so this is worth knowing about even if you never touch EU rules directly."
