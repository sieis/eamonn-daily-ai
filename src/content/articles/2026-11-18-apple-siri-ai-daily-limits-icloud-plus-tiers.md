title: "Apple Spells Out Siri AI's Daily Limits: Bigger iCloud+ Plans Get Up to 20 Times More"
date: 2026-11-18
summary: "A new Apple support document says advanced Siri AI requests, cloud-powered Photos tools and some Shortcuts will have daily limits starting with iOS 27.3. Simple requests stay unlimited, and paying for a larger iCloud+ storage plan multiplies the allowance, from 2 times on the $2.99 plan to 20 times on the $59.99 plan."

keyDetails:
  - "Follows Apple's earlier statement that Siri AI would get usage limits with paid extra access. This is the first time Apple has published how it works"
  - "Limited: advanced Siri AI requests that use Apple's cloud models, Photos tools like Clean Up, Extend and Image Playground, Shortcuts that use Apple's cloud models, and third-party apps built on Apple's cloud AI"
  - "Not limited: simple requests such as setting a timer. Apple's example: texting an address is unlimited, but texting an address with a departure time counts as advanced"
  - "Apple calls the free allowance 'generous' but gives no numbers. Limits are per device and per feature, reset around midnight local time, and unused allowance does not roll over. You get a warning as you approach the cap"
  - "iCloud+ boosts, as reported by Cult of Mac: 200GB ($2.99/month) up to 2x, 2TB ($9.99) up to 5x, 6TB ($29.99) up to 10x, 12TB ($59.99) up to 20x"
  - "The extra allowance is a shared pool across every device on the Apple Account, and family members draw from it first come, first served"
  - "Extra access is not available in Brazil, the EU, the UK or South Korea"
  - "Takes effect with iOS 27.3, now in beta. The real size of the free cap will become clear as testers report it"
whyItMatters: "AI features cost real money to run, and Apple is now joining Google, OpenAI and Microsoft in rationing the expensive ones. For most people, everyday Siri requests won't change. But if you lean on Siri AI or the Photos editing tools a lot, you may hit a daily ceiling, and the way to raise it is to pay for a bigger iCloud storage plan."
consultingAngle: "If your business hands out iPhones or Macs, or your staff use personal Apple devices for work, check how they are tied to Apple Accounts. Because the paid boost is shared across a whole account and a family, one heavy user can drain it for everyone else. Don't upgrade iCloud plans just for this until the free limits are known, since most work tasks may fit easily within them. If your team relies on AI for heavy daily work like long documents, research or image editing, a dedicated business AI tool with clear limits and admin controls is still a more predictable choice than Siri. And if you build an app on Apple's cloud AI, know that your customers' usage now counts against their personal daily limits."
