keyDetails:
  - "Rolling out on Android and iPhone starting November 16. Ask Maps is also expanding to 30 European countries, including the U.K., Germany and Spain"
  - "Trip itineraries: ask for something like a holiday shopping day or a three-day coastal drive and get a day-by-day plan with numbered pins, tips and live details such as traffic and opening hours"
  - "Plans can be edited by chat (add a lunch stop or a viewpoint), shared with others, or exported to Google Docs on desktop"
  - "Appointment finder: covers beauty, pet care, auto and home repair. A request like 'find me a 4 p.m. nail appointment near me' shows open slots across nearby businesses"
  - "Google's AI can also call local businesses on the customer's behalf to ask about availability"
  - "Ask Maps can check which saved places have room for a group during a given time window"
  - "Google has not said which booking systems feed the appointment results. Meta's Muse assistant gained a similar ability to call businesses earlier this month"
whyItMatters: "More people now start their day out, their trip or their search for a haircut by asking an AI instead of scrolling through search results. Google Maps is where a huge share of local discovery already happens, so this changes how customers find and choose small businesses. The AI picks a short list for them, and a business that does not show open times may simply not make that list."
consultingAngle: "If you take appointments, this is the week to check how you look to an AI. Open your Google Business Profile and confirm your hours, services, holiday closures and booking link are correct. If you use online booking software, check whether it connects to Google so your real open slots can show up. Then brief whoever answers your phone: some callers will now be an AI asking whether you have a 4 p.m. opening. Treat it like any customer inquiry and answer clearly. Google has previously let businesses manage automated calls from Google in their Business Profile settings, so look there if you would rather not get them. For restaurants, shops and attractions, make sure your listing has current hours and good photos, because itineraries pull those details straight in."
