consultingAngle: "Use this as a benchmark, not a target. Spending about $16 per employee per month is typical for a paying business, so a few well-chosen tools are enough to be average. The shift toward customer support is worth noticing: answering common questions is an easier first AI project than rebuilding your whole operation, and it can run on cheaper models. Start by listing the 10 questions your customers ask most, then test whether an AI assistant answers them correctly before letting it talk to customers unsupervised. Remember that Ramp's customers skew tech-savvy, so businesses outside that group are likely less far along."
