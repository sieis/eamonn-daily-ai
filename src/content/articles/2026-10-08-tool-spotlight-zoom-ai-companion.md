consultingAngle: "Example: an accounting firm runs a 30-minute call with a client about year-end paperwork. Instead of someone typing notes, AI Companion posts a summary with a list of who promised what by when. The firm pastes the action items into an email to the client the same afternoon, saving 10 to 15 minutes per call. Start by turning it on for one recurring meeting, read the first few summaries against what you remember, and confirm your plan includes it before relying on it. If you prefer another note-taker, such as Fathom or Otter, that works too; the habit of a written recap is the real win."
